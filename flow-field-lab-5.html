Shapes for new scenes and spawns

Every agent is a rigid body. Circles don't rotate. Squares can face their heading through a damped angular spring (a motor that contacts can resist), keep their orientation, or spin freely; facing never changes their speed. Keeping orientation gives the fewest overlaps. Contacts are hard constraints solved by augmented vertex block descent, so agents push each other instead of passing through.

Every action becomes a command that runs 12 ticks (100 ms) after it is issued. Fields are requested every 10 ticks and applied exactly 10 ticks later. Peer B runs the same commands in its own workers, by default with the other neighbour search, a jittery link and late field replies. Both peers hash their full state every 30 ticks.

Controls

  • Left-drag selects agents; Shift adds.
  • Click with agents selected sends them there.
  • Right-drag pans; the wheel zooms. On touch screens, two fingers pan and pinch to zoom.
  • 1–4 tools, B switches neighbour search, R replays peer B, V arrows, H heat, F arrows of the selection, Esc clears, Space pauses, Home fits the map.
