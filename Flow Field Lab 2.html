Controls

  • Left-drag selects agents; Shift adds.
  • Click with agents selected sends them there.
  • Right-drag pans; the wheel zooms. On touch screens, two fingers pan and pinch to zoom.
  • 1–4 tools, V arrows, H heat, F arrows of the selection, G switches CPU and GPU, Esc clears, Space pauses, Home fits the map.
