Hashed grid: one grid level per size tier. Layered BVH: per-layer cell grids of dynamic trees with fat look-ahead boxes, adapted from your DBVH. Both feed the same movement rules.

View

Big agents follow fields built on walls thickened to their size, so they avoid gaps they cannot fit.

Controls

  • Left-drag selects agents; Shift adds.
  • Click with agents selected sends them there.
  • Right-drag pans; the wheel zooms. On touch screens, two fingers pan and pinch to zoom.
  • 1–4 tools, G switches CPU and GPU, B switches neighbour search, V arrows, H heat, F arrows of the selection, Esc clears, Space pauses, Home fits the map.
